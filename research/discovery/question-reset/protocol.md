# Question-first reset — decision protocol

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: literature and public-contract screening; no new experiments.

The preceding measurement pilot failed its advancement gate. We pause defect hunting,
evaluator audits, and new framework implementation. This round compares at most three
questions, starting from an operator decision and the strongest existing answer.

Candidates fixed after initial title/abstract discovery:
1. Which recovery actions remain safe after external deduplication retention expires?
2. Can selective revalidation preserve useful work without retaining invalid authority?
3. What evidence permits safe recovery from an ambiguous external outcome?

For each: specify decision, public grounding, nearest answer, residual uncertainty,
strongest objection, cheapest discriminating experiment, and stop criterion. Discovery
already exposed overlapping results; this is neither a blinded nor systematic review.

Advance only if the question has a consequential supported setting, a precise unresolved
claim after reading the closest sources, an adequate conventional baseline, and a feasible
test whose possible outcomes change a decision. A plausible limitation or missing experiment
in one paper alone is insufficient. Do not force a winner.

All proposed experiments must run in GitHub CI after a separate frozen executable protocol.
This round does not run experiments locally or in CI. Documentation commits may trigger
existing workflows; those are not new scientific trials. No external outreach is authorized.

The deliverable is a comparison and an explicit go/no-go decision, including what evidence
would justify reopening a candidate. Negative selection is an acceptable outcome.
